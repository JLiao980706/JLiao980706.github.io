Fangshuo (Jasper) Liao

I am a PhD candidate in Computer Science at Rice University in Houston, TX, advised by Dr. Anastasios Kyrillidis. I also participate in the AI-OWLS research cluster. Before my PhD, I received a Bachelor of Science in Computer Science and Bachelor of Arts in Mathematics from Rice University in 2020.

My research interest lies broadly in deep learning theory, convex and nonconvex optimization, and developing methods that provably improve the efficiency of solving machine learning problems. I try to answer: What is the theoretical guarantee of an algorithm? What are the mathematical properties of the problem? How can we leverage those properties to design better algorithms?

I am actively looking for a postdoc position starting May–August 2026. Feel free to reach out!

Barbara Su, Fangshuo Liao, Anastasios Kyrillidis

 SGD at the Edge of Stability: The Stochastic Sharpness Gap
Fangshuo Liao, Afroditi Kolomvaki, Anastasios Kyrillidis
arXiv preprint arXiv:2604.21016, 2026
paper

Extends the Edge of Stability phenomenon from full-batch GD to SGD. Introduces "stochastic self-stabilization" and derives a closed-form equation for the sharpness gap, showing smaller batch sizes correlate with flatter solutions.

 Exploiting Low-Rank Objective Structure in Discrete Quadratic Optimization
Ria Stevens, Fangshuo Liao, Barbara Su, Thanasis Hadjidimoulas, Jianqiang Li, Anastasios Kyrillidis
arXiv preprint arXiv:2602.20376, 2026
paper

When the objective matrix has rank r, global maximizers of complex-valued quadratic forms over roots of unity belong to a candidate set of size O(rn2r-1), constructible efficiently via hyperplane arrangement enumeration. A randomized variant scales to problems exceeding one million variables.

Fangshuo Liao, Anastasios Kyrillidis
