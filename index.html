Fangshuo (Jasper) Liao

I am a PhD candidate in Computer Science at Rice University in Houston, TX, advised by Dr. Anastasios Kyrillidis. I also participate in the AI-OWLS research cluster. Before my PhD, I received a Bachelor of Science in Computer Science and Bachelor of Arts in Mathematics from Rice University in 2020.

My research interest lies broadly in deep learning theory, convex and nonconvex optimization, and developing methods that provably improve the efficiency of solving machine learning problems. I try to answer: What is the theoretical guarantee of an algorithm? What are the mathematical properties of the problem? How can we leverage those properties to design better algorithms?

I am actively looking for a postdoc position starting May–August 2026. Feel free to reach out!

 Provable Model-Parallel Distributed Principal Component Analysis with Parallel Deflation
Fangshuo Liao, Wenyi Su, Anastasios Kyrillidis
Conference on Parsimony and Learning (CPAL), 2025
paper / code / poster

A distributed PCA framework where workers each target a distinct eigenvector and refine via hierarchical updates from peer workers, inspired by the deflation method. Provides theoretical convergence analysis of asynchronous updates with low communication cost.

Fangshuo Liao, Anastasios Kyrillidis
International Conference on Algorithmic Learning Theory (ALT), 2024
paper / poster

Introduces a new class of objective functions with "partial strong convexity" and proves Nesterov's momentum achieves acceleration for this class. First work to prove accelerated convergence for deep ReLU neural network architectures.

Fangshuo Liao, J. Lyle Kim, Cruz Barnum, Anastasios Kyrillidis
International Conference on Machine Learning (ICML), 2024
paper / poster

Mathematically characterizes error propagation of the inexact Hotelling's deflation method in PCA. Derives explicit error bounds for both abstract sub-routines and power iteration as the eigenvector-finding procedure.
